# v0.5.0 — A concert agenda for Navidrome

- The offline dashboard groups upcoming shows, recent shows and dates to be
  confirmed. Each listing shows its venue, available times, source links and
  when it was checked.
- Search by artist, show, venue or city. Filter by timing, listing status or
  source. A merged concert can be found under any of its sources.
- Switch to the month calendar to see concert counts by day and full details
  for the selected date. Search follows matching shows across months. Arrow
  keys move the selected day, including across month boundaries.
- Cancelled and rescheduled shows stay in the appropriate timing groups.
  MusicBrainz multi-day shows keep their full range in the dashboard and
  calendar exports.
- Calendar downloads include the full report. Lookup diagnostics sit below the
  agenda, and the obsolete empty Concerts settings tab has been removed.

Replace the installed `navidrome-upcoming-concerts-plugin.ndp` and rescan plugins
or restart Navidrome. Keep plugin storage to preserve scan caches, provider
quotas and calendar state. Reopen the generated `upcoming-concerts.html` to see
the updated dashboard.

The release includes the installable package, source ZIP and SHA-256 checksums.
